# Code of Conduct

All research group members are expected to abide by the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC-CH) policies
which can be found [here](https://handbook.unc.edu/policies.html). Of particular importance are the Honor Code, 
Responsible Conduct of Research, and Research Misconduct.

## Values

The ZETA research group is committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and collaborative environment. 
Towards, we expect you to
- Respect your fellow research group members.
- Respect the privacy and comfort of members by only taking photos or recordings of others with their explicit 
knowledge and consent. Please also obtain consent prior to posting any images on social media.
- Challenge other groups members but in a respectful way.

## Scientific Integrity

We are committed to ensuring scientific integrity and preventing research misconduct. There is no tolerance for 
fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism. Remember the reason why you are working in epidemiology and your personal
mission statement. 

All members are expected to have the relevant CITI and IRB training for the ethical conduct of research. Further, I 
expect research group members to explore training opportunities in the Responsible Conduct of Research. Options are 
available through NC TraCS.

We adhere to the ICMJE authorship guidelines. See the `authortship.md` file for furth details.
