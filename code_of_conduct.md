# Code of Conduct

All research group members are expected to abide by the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill (UNC-CH) policies
which can be found [here](https://handbook.unc.edu/policies.html). Of particular importance are the Honor Code, 
Responsible Conduct of Research, and Research Misconduct.

----

## Values

The ZETA research group is committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and collaborative environment. 
Towards, we expect you to
- Respect your fellow research group members.
- Respect the privacy and comfort of members by only taking photos or recordings of others with their explicit 
knowledge and consent. Please also obtain consent prior to posting any images on social media.
- Challenge other groups members but in a respectful way. Use "I" statements rather than "you" statements when 
providing feedback.

## Scientific Integrity

We are committed to ensuring scientific integrity and preventing research misconduct. There is no tolerance for 
fabrication, falsification, or plagiarism. Remember the reason why you are working in epidemiology and your personal
mission statement. 

All members are expected to have the relevant CITI and IRB training for the ethical conduct of research. Further, I 
expect research group members to explore training opportunities in the Responsible Conduct of Research. Options are 
available through NC TraCS.

We adhere to the ICMJE authorship guidelines. See the `authorship.md` file for further details on guidelines for 
authorship.

----

## Expectations

These are some general expectations of all research group members, including myself:
- Show up on time. Sometimes things happen which prevent timeliness, but do not make a habit of being late.
- Complete your work on the agreed up timeline(s). Again, circumstances may prevent this but clearly communicate and
do not make a habit of delays.
- Do not attend research group meetings if you are sick. Please let me know if you will not attend, but take adequate 
time to rest and return when you are recovered and healthy.
- My working hours are 9-5 EST Monday through Friday. You are welcome to determine your own working hours, but note that
I only expect responses during those hours, and similarly you should only expect responses from me during those hours.
Maintaining an appropriate work-life balance is important.

### PI

I will 
- Review your work in a timely manner. The maximum amount of time will generally be 2 weeks.
- Be timely for our scheduled meetings.
- Do what I can to help you be successful in your career goals.
- Introduce you to different contacts at conferences whom I know.

### PhD students and candidates

Students and candidates are expected to :
- Review all the materials provided at this webpage.
- Adhere to the Code of Conduct.
- Use the `resources/project_checklist` when starting or proposing a new research project.
- Maintain a lab notebook while conducting your research project.
- Disclose use of AI/LLMs in accordance with the policies described in `ai_policy`.
- Admit any mistakes that may happen to me. Mistakes happen, especially when you are learning. Being able to trust each 
other is more important than never making a mistake. Together, we can learn from them. 
- Provide to any co-authors or other parties at least 2 weeks prior to the deadline with written work that has a formal 
deadline. Note that there may be expections for certain dissertation deadlines that may require have different 
deadlines. In those cases, the school or department policy applies.
