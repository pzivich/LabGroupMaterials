# Recommended Readings

This a collection of papers and chapters that are recommended readings for a variety of different topics. The purpose
of this list is to collect different resources for a variety of topics in the group. Please feel free to contribute
additional readings directly or through a GitHub issue. Readings are divided into different topic areas. Feel free to 
also suggest new topic areas.

---

## Professional Development

- August ET & Brouwer AF. (2024). How to write an effective journal peer review using a staged writing approach: a 
best-practice guide for early-career researchers. *International Journal of Epidemiology*, 53(6), dyae154.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39570678/
- Yanai I. (2026). How to give a bad talk. *Nature Reviews Cancer*, 1-1. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42342955/

## Epidemiologic Concepts

- Banack HR et al. (2025). Collider stratification bias II: magnitude of bias. *American Journal of Epidemiology*, 
194(5), 1149-1151. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39108158/
- Liu et al. (2012). Implications of M bias in epidemiologic studies: a simulation study. 
*American Journal of Epidemiology*, 176(10), 938-948. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23100247/

## Statistical Methods

- Cole SR, Chu H, & Greenland S. (2014). Maximum likelihood, profile likelihood, and penalized likelihood: a primer. 
*American Journal of Epidemiology*, 179(2), 252-260. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24173548/
- Hamra G, MacLehose R, & Richardson D. (2013). Markov chain Monte Carlo: an introduction for epidemiologists. 
*International Journal of Epidemiology*, 42(2), 627-634. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23569196/
- Kulesa A et al. (2015). Sampling distributions and the bootstrap: the bootstrap can be used to assess uncertainty of 
sample estimates. *Nature Methods*, 12(6), 477. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26221652/
- Morris TP, White IR, & Crowther MJ. (2019). Using simulation studies to evaluate statistical methods. 
*Statistics in Medicine*, 38(11), 2074-2102. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30652356/
- Ross RK, Zivich PN, Stringer JS, & Cole SR. (2024). M-estimation for common epidemiological measures: introduction 
and applied examples. *International Journal of Epidemiology*, 53(2), dyae030. 
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38423105/
