The following provides a checklist of some recommended items for new students or students new to working with me. Note
that these are suggestions and are subject to modifications. These items also go beyond the program requirements, but 
all of those requirements still apply. If you think there are items I am missing (that would be beneficial to include), 
please let me know or create a pull request on GitHub.

## Checklist

Accounts
- [ ] Registered for an [ORCiD](https://orcid.org/) 
- [ ] Registered for a [GitHub](https://github.com/) account
- [ ] Registered for a [PubMed](https://account.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/signup/) account

Responsible Conduct of Research (RCR)

- [ ] Enrolled in the July 3-day RCR course through [NC TraCS](https://tracs.unc.edu/index.php/services/education/responsible-conduct-of-research)
- [ ] Completed CITI training through [UNC](https://clinicalresearch.unc.edu/resources/training-and-education/citi-trainings/)
