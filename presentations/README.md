# ZETA Research Group Presentations

Collection of short continuing eduction presentations presented at group meetings. These are intended to highlight
information that do not regularly come up in coursework or your training. However, I think they are important pieces 
of information for you to know.

---

## Files

`images/`
- Images used when compiling the presentations from a `.tex` file into a `.pdf`.

`intro_to_arxiv.*`
- Introduction to the pre-print server arXiv.

`predatory_publishers.*`
- Discussion of predatory journals and predatory conferences, including how to identify them.

`responding_to_reviewers.*`
- Recommendations for how to respond to reviewers when resubmitting a paper to an academic journal.
